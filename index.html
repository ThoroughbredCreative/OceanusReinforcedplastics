Vacuum Infusion Manufacturing

Resin is drawn through dry fibre under vacuum, producing parts with a consistent resin-to-fibre ratio.

Vacuum infusion suits structural or weight-sensitive parts where consistency and strength-to-weight matter, and works well for both one-off components and repeat runs from a shared mold.

  • Lighter, more consistent parts than open hand layup
  • Reduced resin waste and cleaner shop environment
  • Well suited to structural marine and industrial components

Hand Layup Composites

Resin and fibreglass are applied by hand for maximum flexibility on shape and detail.

Hand layup is the right fit for custom geometry, small production runs, and structural repairs where a mold isn't practical or available.

  • Ideal for custom shapes and one-off parts
  • Practical for structural repairs and patch work
  • No mold required for many repair jobs

About Us

Composites manufacturing, done to spec

Oceanus Reinforced Plastics specializes in semi-custom composites manufacturing, via hand layup and/or vacuum infusion.

Working out of North Saanich Marina puts us in daily contact with boat owners, builders and marine trades who need composite parts made right — whether that's a single repair or a run of matching components.

We keep both manufacturing methods in-house so the process fits the part, not the other way around.

Right method for the part

We recommend hand layup or vacuum infusion based on your part's shape, strength needs and volume.

Direct communication

You work directly with the people manufacturing your part, from quote to delivery.

Built to spec

Every part is semi-custom — manufactured against your requirements, not a fixed catalogue.
